PHASE 14 - SHOULDER ALIGNMENT
Built from the user's uploaded Phase 13 project ZIP and the latest webcam screenshot.

Screenshot observation:
- The neck opening/collar placement is substantially improved.
- The blue garment's upper shoulder seams sit inward from the person's actual
  shoulder landmarks, leaving too much of the original dark shirt exposed.

Code findings:
- The 7-column source mesh is non-uniform, but destination horizontal control
  coordinates were still generated from a uniform -1..1 sequence. This means
  source and destination control columns did not correspond, pinching/misplacing
  the shoulder and collar region.
- The upper shoulder seam controls were not explicitly attached to landmarks 11
  and 12.

Changes:
- Destination horizontal coordinates now derive from the same normalized
  non-uniform source-column positions.
- Added explicit left/right shoulder seam anchors at mesh row 2, columns 1 and 5,
  pinned to MediaPipe landmarks 11 and 12.
- Added a gentle downward continuation into the next mesh row.
- Shoulder pins are applied after neckline smoothing so the collar adjustment
  cannot pull them away from the tracked shoulders.
- Neckline geometry, transparent T-shirt asset, camera pipeline, and tracking
  remain unchanged.

Validation:
- Backend unit tests: 8/8 passed.
- ZIP integrity checked.
- Actual webcam appearance and FPS still need confirmation on the user's PC.

Scope:
- This phase targets upper shoulder placement only. It does not yet remove all
  original dark fabric from the sleeves or guarantee photorealistic replacement.
