PHASE 9 - NECKLINE ANCHOR REBUILD

Why this changes the method:
- The previous 5x5 mesh sampled the 760px garment image at uniform vertical
  intervals. It had no control point near the collar cut-out (roughly y=145px),
  so lifting the top mesh row did not correctly move the neckline.
- This build uses a 7x7 mesh with non-uniform source rows around the collar and
  non-uniform columns around the collar boundaries.
- The collar row is now independently pulled toward a neck-base anchor derived
  from the shoulder midpoint and torso vertical axis; adjacent controls move
  less to create a rounded transition.
- Retains the narrow transparent-neck-opening cleanup from Phase 7.
- Backend app explicitly instantiates the 7x7 mesh.

Validation:
- Backend unit tests: 6/6 passed.
- ZIP integrity checked.
- This does not guarantee a specific webcam appearance or FPS on the user's PC.

Limitations:
- This is still a 2D pose-driven overlay, not full clothing segmentation.
- The original black shirt may remain visible at sleeves and side edges.
- A 7x7 mesh is heavier than 5x5; benchmark webcam FPS after installation.
