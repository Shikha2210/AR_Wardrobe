PHASE 22 - SHOULDER SLEEVE RESTORE FIX
Built from Phase 21 and the latest annotated webcam screenshot.

Diagnosis:
The arm foreground restoration mask started at each shoulder landmark and
used a thick line (17% of shoulder width). This restored original camera
pixels over the shoulder cap, bringing the user's dark T-shirt sleeve back
on top of the blue virtual shirt. This explains the persistent dark patches
circled by the user; simply widening the garment mesh could not fix them.

Changes:
- Extracted arm foreground mask generation into _arm_foreground_mask().
- Start restoration at 22% of the shoulder-to-elbow segment instead of at the
  shoulder landmark.
- Narrow upper-arm restoration stroke from 17% to 10.5% of shoulder width.
- Narrow forearm restoration stroke from 11.5% to 7.5%.
- Keep the lower arm/skin in front while allowing the virtual T-shirt to cover
  the shoulder/sleeve cap.
- Preserved person segmentation, shoulder/chest mesh, neckline, waist and hem.

Validation:
- Backend unit tests: 12/12 passed.
- Python syntax and ZIP integrity checked.
- Live webcam output and FPS are not yet verified on the user's computer.

Expected tradeoff:
The virtual tee should cover more of the original dark sleeve at the shoulder.
Because the arm mask is narrower, unusual arm poses may show a small amount of
garment overlap onto the upper arm; inspect front-facing, arms-relaxed and
arms-out poses.
