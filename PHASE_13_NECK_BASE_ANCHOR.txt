PHASE 13 - NECK BASE ANCHOR (BUILT FROM THE USER'S UPLOADED PHASE 12 ZIP)

What inspection found:
- Phase 12 still positioned the collar row by subtracting a fixed percentage
  from a row interpolated between the shoulder midpoint and hip midpoint.
- That does not identify the neck base, so the collar can remain on the upper
  chest even when the percentage is increased.
- The bundled PNG already has a transparent centre opening; alpha at the
  opening centre is 0 and the shirt body below it is opaque. Therefore the
  main remaining issue in this uploaded build is placement, not a missing
  transparent opening.

Change:
- Replaced the percentage-only centre lift with explicit left, centre and
  right collar anchors derived from shoulder midpoint, shoulder width and
  torso axis.
- Estimated neck base is placed above the shoulder midpoint.
- The centre collar point sits slightly lower than the two side points to
  produce a shallow crew-neck U curve.
- Blended the anchor corrections into the next mesh row to reduce kinks.
- Kept the existing 7x7 mesh, asset and websocket pipeline.

Validation:
- Backend unit tests: 7/7 passed.
- Python compilation of app.py, tracking.py and tryon.py passed.
- ZIP integrity checked.
- Webcam visual result and FPS have NOT been verified on the user's computer.

Limitations:
- Neck base is estimated from pose geometry because MediaPipe Pose does not
  provide a dedicated neck-base landmark.
- This is still a 2D overlay, not photorealistic cloth simulation or full
  semantic replacement of the original shirt.
